About the Society

The Oxford Salsa Society is a vibrant community of students, staff, and locals who share a love for salsa dancing. From absolute beginners to seasoned pros, we provide a friendly and welcoming atmosphere for everyone to learn, practice, and enjoy the rich culture of Latin dance. Our goal is to build confidence, create friendships, and most importantly, have fun on the dance floor!
